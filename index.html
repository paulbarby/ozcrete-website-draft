What we do

Four services that cover the life of an aquatic facility, from the first drawing to the last service call.

Construction

Commercial pools & aquatic centres

Full construction of commercial aquatic facilities — filtration plant, hydraulics, structures and finishes — for councils, schools, developers and clubs. We build the plant room we have to maintain later, which changes how it gets built.

Maintenance

Maintenance & repairs

Pools take a hammering over a Queensland summer. Scheduled servicing, minor repairs and breakdown response for commercial facilities that cannot afford to be closed.

Filtration

Filtration systems

Complete end-to-end commercial filtration solutions — design, supply, install and commissioning. Built around the actual bather load and the actual water, not a catalogue number.

Consultation

Consultation & project advice

Bring us in early and we will tell you what the design will cost you in maintenance for the next twenty years. Experience makes the difference, and it is cheaper before the concrete goes in.

The commercial building industry has a gap in it — aquatic work gets handed to builders who have never run a plant room. Ozcrete has spent thirty years filling that gap.
